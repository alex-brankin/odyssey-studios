Privacy Policy

Applies to: Miner Tycoon, Bee Tycoon and Blzn Studios (Odyssey) Discord apps

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Contact: alexanderbrankin@gmail.com

Who we are

These Discord apps are operated by Odyssey Studios ("we", "us"). This policy

explains what data the apps collect, why, how long we keep it, and how to have it

removed.

What we collect

We only collect data from Discord servers our apps have been added to. We do not

collect anything from servers we are not in, and we do not collect direct messages

between users.

All three apps

  • Discord user IDs and usernames — to identify who ran a command, who holds a

role, and who appears on a leaderboard.

  • Server, channel and role IDs — to know where to post and which roles to

apply.

  • Command usage — which command was run, when, and by whom, for rate limiting

and abuse prevention.

Miner Tycoon only

  • **Message content, author ID, author display name, channel, timestamp and

message link**, stored in a local database. This powers the server's chat

analytics, moderation logging and the staff insights features.

  • Message content sent to Google Gemini. Some messages are passed to Google's

Gemini API to generate staff summaries and to detect questions the bot can answer

automatically. Google processes this content on our behalf under their API terms.

We do not use it to train any model of our own, and we do not permit it to be

used to train Google's.

  • Moderation records — edited and deleted message content, ban reasons and

nickname history, so moderators can review actions after the fact.

  • Member join and leave events, used for server growth reporting.
  • Epic Games in-game names that users voluntarily submit to link their account

to the leaderboard.

Bee Tycoon and Blzn Studios (Odyssey)

  • Leaderboard submissions — screenshots or scores users upload, together with

the submitting user's ID, synced to a private Google Sheet.

  • Notification subscriptions — which game-update roles a user has opted into.
  • Upload counts per user, to enforce daily submission limits.

What we never collect

  • Payment or financial information.
  • Passwords or Discord authentication tokens belonging to users.
  • Data about a user's presence, status or activity. Our apps do not use the

Presence Intent.

  • Message content in servers where the app has not been added.

Why we need privileged intents

  • Message Content Intent — our apps use text commands (!, +, OS!

prefixes) which cannot function without it. Miner Tycoon additionally uses

message content for its moderation log and server analytics features, which are

the core reason that server uses the app.

  • Server Members Intent — needed for role assignment via reactions, join and

leave reporting, leaderboard role management, and member counts. The cached

member list is also what lets the apps resolve a user ID to a member without

making an API call for every lookup.

We do not request the Presence Intent and do not read user presence.

How long we keep it

  • Message logs and analytics: 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • Moderation records: 12 months, as they may be needed to review a past action.
  • Leaderboard entries and subscriptions: kept until the user asks for removal, or

until they leave the server.

  • Everything else: deleted when the app is removed from the server.

Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade user data, and we do not share it for advertising.

Data is shared only with the services needed to run the apps:

  • Google Sheets — leaderboard submissions, in a private sheet accessible only

to server staff.

  • Google Gemini API — message content from the Miner Tycoon server, for the

staff summaries and automatic answers described above.

  • Our hosting provider — the apps run on a private server where this data is

stored.

We may disclose data if required by law, or to investigate abuse of our services.

Security

Data is stored on access-controlled hosting. Credentials are kept in environment

variables, not in source code. Access is limited to the app operators.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you what data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it.

Email alexanderbrankin@gmail.com from an address we can tie to your Discord account, or

contact a server administrator, and we will action it within 30 days.

Removing the app from a server, or leaving that server, stops all further

collection about you in it.

Children

Our apps are not directed at anyone under 13, in line with Discord's Terms of

Service. If we learn we hold data about a child under 13, we delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and announce it in the

servers the apps are in.
